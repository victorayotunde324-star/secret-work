Privacy Policy

Secret Work respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the Secret Work website and application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect account information such as your name, email address, login information, subscription status, and preferences. We may also receive technical information such as device type, operating system, IP address, app activity, and diagnostic data.

2. Payments and Subscriptions

Payments are processed through the relevant app store or payment provider. Secret Work does not need to store your full payment-card number to provide subscription access. Payment providers may process transaction information under their own privacy policies.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain Secret Work services.
  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide subscription access and customer support.
  • Improve features, content, performance, and user experience.
  • Monitor security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Send service-related communications and, where permitted, product updates.

4. Analytics

We may use analytics and similar technologies to understand how users interact with the service, measure performance, and improve the product. Where third-party analytics providers are used, their processing is subject to their respective policies.

5. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help operate the platform, such as hosting, analytics, authentication, customer-support, and payment providers. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect rights, safety, or the integrity of the service.

7. Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, subject to applicable law and operational requirements. You may also manage certain communications through the settings or instructions provided with those communications.

8. Children's Privacy

Secret Work is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so the information can be reviewed and, where appropriate, deleted.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the official Secret Work support contact associated with your account or app listing.
